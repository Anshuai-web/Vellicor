Privacy Policy & Notice at Collection

CCPA / CPRA (Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.100) • DOPPA (6 Del. C. § 1201C) • Zero-Retention AI Standard

3.1 Notice at Collection for Business Inquiries

Vellicor Technologies Ltd collects business contact information submitted voluntarily through our Executive Briefing form (such as full name, corporate email address, employer/company name, professional job title, and inquiry text). We collect this information solely to evaluate enterprise modernization requests, schedule architectural briefings, and communicate regarding prospective B2B commercial engagements.

3.2 "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" Declaration

STATUTORY DECLARATION: Vellicor Technologies Ltd does NOT sell personal information and does NOT share personal information with third parties for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), and related US state privacy statutes (including Delaware DOPPA, Colorado CPA, Virginia VCDPA), consumers have the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of their personal information. Because Vellicor never sells or shares personal data with data brokers or advertising networks, no opt-out is required. For inquiries, contact privacy@vellicortech.com.

3.3 Consumer Privacy Rights

Depending on your state of residence, you may have the statutory right to:

  • Right to Know / Access: Request disclosure of categories and specific pieces of personal information collected.
  • Right to Deletion: Request deletion of personal information, subject to statutory recordkeeping exceptions.
  • Right to Correction: Request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: Exercise your privacy rights without receiving discriminatory treatment or diminished service quality.

To exercise any of these rights, submit a verifiable consumer request to privacy@vellicortech.com. We verify requests within 45 days in compliance with statutory timelines.

3.4 Sovereign AI & Zero Data Egress Architecture

In delivering our software architectures, Vellicor deploys enterprise AI models strictly within client-governed sovereign Virtual Private Clouds (VPCs) or dedicated private hardware enclaves. Under no circumstances is client proprietary data, prompt telemetry, source code, or internal database records ingested, retained, or utilized to train or fine-tune public multi-tenant models.
