Privacy Policy & Data Governance

Our Commitment to Protecting Your Information

2.1 Information We Collect

We collect business contact information that you voluntarily choose to submit through our Executive Briefing form, email inquiries, or direct correspondence. This may include your full name, business email address, company or organization name, role or job title, and details regarding your technical inquiry or modernization initiative.

2.2 How We Use Collected Information

Information submitted to us is used strictly to evaluate your inquiry, schedule technical or executive working sessions, provide requested architectural information, and coordinate professional consulting discussions. We do not use this information for unsolicited consumer marketing.

2.3 We Do Not Sell or Monetize Your Personal Data

COMMITMENT: Vellicor Technologies Ltd does not sell, rent, or lease personal information to third parties, data brokers, or advertising networks.

We respect your privacy. We do not participate in behavioral tracking or cross-context advertising networks. Your business inquiry details remain confidential and are shared only internally with authorized personnel necessary to respond to your request.

2.4 Client Data Isolation Best Practices

In our consulting practice and software architectures, we advocate for client-governed isolation. When assisting enterprise clients with AI pipelines or integration platforms, models and datasets are deployed within client-governed virtual private clouds (VPCs) or dedicated infrastructure. Client proprietary data and internal code repositories are never retained or used to train public multi-tenant models.

2.5 Security Measures & Data Rights

We maintain administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal and business information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. If you wish to review, update, or request the deletion of contact details previously submitted, please send your request to admin@vellicortech.com.
